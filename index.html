About this tool

This is my private initiative to make life easier, based on daily experience from more than 20 years in the lighting industry.

Drop an IES, LDT or GLDF file and see the light effect straight away, with no calculation model to prepare. It shows direct light only, computed per pixel from the photometric file.

Everything runs in your browser. Your files are never uploaded anywhere.
